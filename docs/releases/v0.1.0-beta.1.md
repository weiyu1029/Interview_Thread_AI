# InterviewThread v0.1.0-beta.1

> Founding-beta release notes. InterviewThread is beta software: verify important
> outputs and never place private career data or credentials in public issues.

## Founding-beta objective

This release opens the complete evidence-to-interview journey to a small cohort
of candidates and interview professionals. The goal is to find inaccurate,
repetitive, confusing, inaccessible, or blocked experiences before expanding
access—not to claim that the product is finished.

## What beta testers can evaluate

- add a real resume or truthful career evidence and one job post;
- review source-linked evidence matches and genuine gaps;
- turn supported experience into defensible interview stories;
- practise role-specific follow-up questions in coaching or realistic mode;
- compare the guest and signed-in experience;
- test responsive layouts, language selection, voice input, and question
  playback on supported browsers.

## Evidence and privacy boundary

- No source, no claim: a missing qualification remains a visible gap.
- The product must not invent tools, employers, responsibilities, outcomes,
  numbers, credentials, or authorship.
- The beta application does not request a resume or job description.
- Guest-mode interview history and progress are not saved to an account.
- Real resumes, private job posts, interview transcripts, voice recordings,
  email addresses, and credentials must never be placed in a public GitHub
  issue, discussion, test, or pull request.

## Known beta limitations

- Eight locale catalogs have been reviewed end to end; the other 32 locale
  catalogs remain community-beta translations.
- Voice quality and speech recognition depend on the selected language, browser,
  operating system, microphone permissions, and available device voices.
- Google, GitHub, and LinkedIn sign-in require correct deployment-specific OAuth
  credentials and exact callback URLs.
- Job recommendations and market insights use approved or user-supplied data
  sources; they are not a promise that every live vacancy is indexed.
- The open-source project does not auto-submit applications or send outreach.

## Fifteen-minute verification path

1. Open [InterviewThread](https://interviewthreadai.com/en) in a supported browser.
2. Complete the resume and job-post inputs with truthful test content.
3. Confirm that each strong match links to source evidence and each unsupported
   requirement remains a gap.
4. Complete one mock-interview topic and one follow-up question.
5. Repeat the critical path in guest mode or on a second viewport when practical.
6. Send account-specific feedback through the
   [private contact form](https://interviewthreadai.com/en/contact), or use the
   [beta issue form](https://github.com/weiyu1029/Interview_Thread_AI/issues/new?template=beta_feedback.yml)
   with synthetic data only.

## Before expanding the beta cohort

- [ ] CI, lint, unit tests, and production web build pass.
- [ ] Sign-in, guest mode, document import, evidence mapping, and mock interview
      pass desktop and mobile smoke tests.
- [ ] English and the reviewed locale catalogs have no clipped or untranslated
      critical-path copy.
- [ ] No open P0 or P1 security, privacy, data-loss, sign-in, import, evidence,
      or mock-interview regression remains.
- [ ] Repository description, website URL, topics, social preview, README,
      release notes, support links, and security links use InterviewThread.
- [ ] The release is marked as a pre-release and links to the founding-beta guide.

## Compatibility

Legacy environment-variable and Python-module identifiers remain available for
self-hosted installations. Their presence is compatibility support, not public
product branding.

## Feedback priority

- **P0:** security, privacy, or data loss;
- **P1:** blocks sign-in, import, evidence mapping, or mock interviews;
- **P2:** materially harms accuracy, language, voice, accessibility, or UX;
- **P3:** suggestion or minor polish.

Read the full [founding-beta guide](../BETA_TESTING.md) before publishing or
expanding the cohort.
