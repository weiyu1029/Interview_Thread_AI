# InterviewThread Product Strategy

## Brand promise

**Turn one job description and your real experience into interview stories you
can defend.**

InterviewThread finds the candidate's strongest evidence, exposes the real gaps,
and drills role-specific follow-ups without inventing achievements.

## Wedge product: Interview Proof Pack

The first product sells one outcome. A candidate provides:

1. one real resume or evidence profile;
2. one real job description;
3. an interview date or current application stage.

Within about ten minutes, InterviewThread produces:

1. three strongest role-match proofs, each linked to source evidence;
2. three real capability or evidence gaps;
3. three to five defensible interview stories;
4. ten likely role-specific follow-up questions;
5. one focused 30-minute interview preparation plan.

The primary journey is deliberately linear:

`Resume + JD → Evidence Map → 3 Interview Stories → Mock Interview`

## Product boundary

InterviewThread may reframe evidence, organize it, and use language from the job
description when the candidate's source material supports it. It may not invent
tools, ownership, employers, projects, credentials, results, numbers, or
authorship claims. Missing support remains visible as a gap.

## Differentiation

Most career AI products optimize applications or generate polished answers.
InterviewThread prepares the candidate to defend a truthful answer when a real
interviewer asks the next question.

The defensibility loop is the product moat:

1. every suggestion links to candidate evidence;
2. every unsupported requirement remains a visible gap;
3. every story is tested through role-specific follow-ups;
4. every practice answer can be traced back to the evidence map.

## Ideal initial users

- candidates with a real interview or active application;
- international students preparing for unfamiliar interview conventions;
- career switchers who need to translate transferable evidence honestly;
- candidates in business, analytics, product, operations, strategy, consulting,
  and AI/data roles;
- university career centers and coaches evaluating evidence-grounded workflows.

## Secondary capabilities

Global job recommendations, market insights, application tracking, model
selection, and collaboration remain available inside the product. They do not
compete with the homepage call to action or redefine the first-session outcome.

## Open-source launch

The initial edition is free and open source. The public homepage contains no
paid plan comparison and makes no automatic-application promise. Community
contributions should strengthen evidence accuracy, privacy, accessibility,
localization, and interview quality before adding commercial surface area.

## Future commercial path

Commercialization begins only after the Interview Proof Pack demonstrates
repeatable user value. Potential paid layers include persistent history,
advanced practice analytics, coach or career-center collaboration, team
administration, and enterprise governance. The evidence boundary remains the
same at every access level.

## Success measures

- percentage of new users who complete one evidence map;
- percentage who produce at least three defensible stories;
- percentage who start a mock interview after viewing the pack;
- user-rated confidence before and after the 30-minute preparation flow;
- unsupported-claim rate, which should remain zero;
- repeat use for a second real job description.
